Run a quiz show without a score sheet

MindStakes is a tool for the host's computer. It draws the category and the questions, keeps the answer timer, tracks every team's score and runs three different game modes. Players watch one screen and answer out loud — nobody has to install anything.

Instead of a notepad, a pen and a running tally in your head, you get one window: the current round, the category, the question, the answers, the clock and every team's score.

Three game modes

Betting

The team picks a category, or rerolls it once per round. Everyone bets up to 20 points, the host reveals the answers, and after the check each team gains or loses exactly what it bet.

Fraud

The answering team says whether they know the answer. If nobody challenges, they take 20 points — whether they actually knew it or not. If someone challenges, the stakes turn around: 20 points for the answerer, or 10 for the challenger.

Sabotage

You can answer yourself or hand the question to another team. A correct answer is +20, a wrong one is −20, and passing the question shifts points for both teams — which is why giving away a question you don't know sometimes pays off.

What else it does

  • draws categories and questions so nothing repeats during a game,
  • times the answers and shows the countdown on a progress bar,
  • supports questions with images, audio and video,
  • tracks several teams with their own colours and pictures,
  • saves the game so you can come back after a break,
  • exports the whole configuration with questions and media, so a tournament can move to another machine,
  • has a host panel on F10: reroll the question or switch the answering team without restarting the game.

Your own questions

The built-in creator adds a question, its category, difficulty, supported game modes and media. Questions live outside the project in your own folder — or in any folder you point the game at, including a network drive. Example questions are included, so you can play right after downloading.

Platforms

  • Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit),
  • Linux x86_64,
  • macOS 11 or newer (Intel and Apple Silicon).

The game runs at 1920×1080, needs no internet connection and no extra runtime — everything ships in the download.

Languages

The interface is available in Polish and English and switches with one click. Question text stays in whatever language you write it in.
